# ACA Verify Candidate Safety Hold

Status: **ACTIVE**

ACA Verify is a candidate declared-predicate interpreter. The current code may
be reviewed and tested with synthetic fixtures. It is not authorized for:

- a release or version tag;
- deployment, distribution, or a hosted service;
- pilots, demonstrations using real cases, or consequential decisions;
- marketing claims that it verifies facts, authority, legality, or safety; or
- deletion of the reviewed branches or rewriting their history.

The hold may be lifted only by a recorded human decision after all of these
gates are satisfied:

1. Intellectual-property and public-disclosure boundary review.
2. Licensing review, including the repository's patent-license boundary.
3. Security and adversarial-input review.
4. Claims review confirming that partial declared-predicate conformance is not
   represented as factual, legal, or authority verification.
5. Release approval naming the exact commit and intended use.

Passing automated tests does not lift this hold. A pull request merge does not
lift this hold. Verification is not authorization.
