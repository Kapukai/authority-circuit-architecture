# Projection Architecture

A projection may select, order, and display canonical nodes and edges. It may
add view metadata and non-substantive findings.

A projection must not create new authority facts, suppress counter-authority,
rewrite quotations, infer authorization from verification, or convert
uncertainty into support.

Cross-view reconciliation compares canonical identifiers, source bindings,
states, temporal intervals, and challenge status. Any mismatch is emitted as a
defect signal.
