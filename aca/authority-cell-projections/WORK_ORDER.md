# ACA-121–127 Work Order

## Objective
Define and verify multiple projections of one ACA-120 Verifiable Authority Cell.

## Acceptance criteria
- Stable canonical node and edge IDs.
- Exact quotation separated from interpretation.
- Every derivation edge has an explicit support state.
- Every projection references canonical IDs.
- Cross-view inconsistencies are surfaced.
- Challenges and counter-authority remain visible.
- Historical records are preserved.
- Verification never claims legal truth or permission to act.

## Out of scope
Legal conclusions, autonomous authorization, aggregate lawfulness scores,
silent conflict resolution, and real-case adjudication.
