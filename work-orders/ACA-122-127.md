# ACA-122 through ACA-127 — Authority Cell Projection Packages

Status: PROPOSED

## ACA-122 — Sovereign-to-Action Derivation Projection

Trace a claimed exercise of authority backward and forward through:

People or sovereign source → constitution or foundational instrument → allocated power → enacted law → delegation → jurisdiction → case-specific predicates → procedure → authorizing instrument → identified actor → scoped execution → affected person, right, record, property, or status → review and remedy.

Deliverables include the derivation-view specification, required node and edge classes, completeness rules, explicit unsupported-transition state, and synthetic reference cases.

## ACA-123 — Verbatim Source and Citation Binding

Bind every material authority claim to an inspectable source object containing exact quotation, citation locator, official publication identity, jurisdiction, version, effective interval, retrieval time, source hash, access status, and supersession status.

Exact quotation SHALL remain distinct from interpretation, machine parsing, and human determination. Secondary sources may aid discovery but SHALL NOT silently substitute for controlling primary authority.

## ACA-124 — Transition Proof Obligations

Make every material arrow in the derivation circuit independently inspectable. Each transition record identifies source node, target node, proposition asserted, required conditions, supporting authority, limiting or contrary authority, exceptions, temporal scope, reviewer rationale, and state.

Baseline states: SUPPORTED, UNSUPPORTED, DISPUTED, INDETERMINATE, SUPERSEDED, and OUT_OF_SCOPE.

## ACA-125 — Multi-View Projection and Cross-View Reconciliation

Define derivation, citation, evidence, timeline, challenge, authorization, audit, remedy, and AIM measurement as deterministic projections of the same canonical Authority Cell.

A projection manifest SHALL identify canonical cell version, transformation name and version, generated time, output hash, omitted protected fields, and verification result.

Cross-view checks SHALL surface contradictions such as a cited delegation effective after execution, an unsigned instrument represented as authenticated, or authorization absent while execution is recorded.

## ACA-126 — Challenge, Counter-Authority, and Disagreement Records

Represent challenges without deleting or overwriting the challenged claim. A challenge may identify missing authority, contradictory evidence, alternative interpretation, superseded law, conflict of laws, identity mismatch, temporal defect, procedural defect, jurisdictional dispute, or affected-rights concern.

Independent reviewers' agreements and disagreements SHALL remain separately attributable and reproducible.

## ACA-127 — Temporal and Audit Projection

Render authority grants, amendments, delegations, appointments, predicates, notices, decisions, authorizations, executions, challenges, corrections, revocations, appeals, and remedies in time order.

Every material mutation SHALL record actor, role, time, prior version, new version, reason, authority to modify, affected objects, and integrity metadata. Corrections SHALL append history rather than rewrite it.

## Shared acceptance boundary

These packages define observable records and transformations. They do not establish that a legal interpretation is correct, create authority, authorize action, or replace independent professional and affected-rights review.
