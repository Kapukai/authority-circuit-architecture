ACA-100

A closed-loop authority architecture

Six layers keep authority, evidence, evaluation, proof, and correction distinguishable.

  1. 01Source authority

    Identify source, scope, delegate, jurisdiction, effective period, and revocation status.

  2. 02Formal rule

    Express applicable requirements as declared, typed predicates and dependencies.

  3. 03Evidence

    Reference provenance, integrity metadata, access controls, and unresolved conflicts.

  4. 04Evaluation

    Apply a versioned decision profile without concealing human judgment as computation.

  5. 05Decision proof

    Produce a portable record that supports inspection and lawful replay.

  6. 06Remedy execution

    Detect, notify, preserve, review, correct, propagate, verify, and close.

ACA-140

The decision travels with its proof.

A Decision Proof Record distinguishes integrity proof from truth proof and preserves the material basis for replay.

01

Identity

Record, profile, rule version, evaluator, and evaluation time.

02

Predicate record

Material value, source, evidence reference, unknown, conflict, expiry, or revocation.

03

State transition

Prior state, resulting state, trigger, actor or service, and reason.

04

Remedy route

Available correction path, responsible authority, deadline, and verification status.

ACA-150

Review is an event. Remedy requires verified correction.

  1. Detect
  2. Notify
  3. Preserve
  4. Review
  5. Correct
  6. Propagate
  7. Verify
  8. Close

Closure requires evidence that corrective action occurred and, where applicable, propagated to dependent records and downstream decisions.
