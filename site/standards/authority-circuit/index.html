Authority Circuit Architecture

A formal architecture for translating legal, organizational, and policy authority into deterministic, inspectable decision structures with attached proof and remedy.

1. SourcePublished rule and authority.
2. FormalizeTyped predicates and dependencies.
3. EvaluateDeterministic state transition.
4. ProveReplayable Decision Proof Record.
5. RemedyObservable correction and closure.

The reusable primitive

A Verifiable Authority Cell evaluates authority, jurisdiction, rule applicability, evidence, procedure, time, notice, conflict, and revocation without collapsing uncertainty into a false binary.
