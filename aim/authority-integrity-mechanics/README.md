# Authority Integrity Mechanics

Authority Integrity Mechanics (AIM) is a non-normative research program for discovering measurable engineering properties of exercised authority.

AIM asks:

> Can a proposed exercise of authority be structurally supported under the stated evidence, burden, uncertainty, reversibility, and remedy conditions?

AIM does not determine what is lawful, issue authorization, replace accountable judgment, or convert research findings into standards automatically.

## Program sequence

- `AIM-0000` — Program Charter and Research Boundary
- `AIM-0001` — Measurement Primitives
- `AIM-0002` — Experimental Protocols and Reference Cases
- `AIM-0003` — Reproducibility and Adversarial Validation
- `ACA-200` — Possible future standardization of mechanics that survive validation

## Governing invariant

Hard safety gates precede profiles. Critical failures may not be averaged away.

## Current state

`AIM-0000` is a research candidate. No AIM quantity, profile, model, or conclusion is normative until it has passed reproducibility, adversarial review, expert review, and an explicit human standardization decision.
