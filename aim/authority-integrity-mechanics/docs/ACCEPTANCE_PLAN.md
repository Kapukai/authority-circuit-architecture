# AIM-0000 Acceptance Plan

AIM-0000 may be accepted only after the Human Chief Architect confirms:

1. The program is explicitly research-only and non-normative.
2. The charter separates observation, measurement, synthesis, verification, and human determination.
3. The non-goals prohibit legal conclusions, autonomous authorization, human-worth ranking, and aggregate lawfulness scoring.
4. The hard-gate rule prevents critical failures from being averaged away.
5. The construct register labels every construct as candidate or hypothesis.
6. Promotion to ACA requires reproducibility, adversarial review, expert review, misuse review, and a separate standardization decision.
7. AIM-0001 remains a separate work order and does not begin merely because AIM-0000 files exist.
8. The repository contains no operational AIM scoring engine.

## Acceptance record

Acceptance should be recorded in the pull request with:

- reviewed commit SHA;
- verification result;
- known limitations;
- explicit statement that acceptance authorizes only the research charter, not use of any mechanic in an individual decision.
