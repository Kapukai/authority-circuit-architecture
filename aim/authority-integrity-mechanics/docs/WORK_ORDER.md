# AIM-0000 Work Order — Program Charter

## Authorized objective

Establish the minimum research governance, vocabulary boundaries, promotion gates, and misuse controls required before AIM-0001 may define measurement primitives.

## Inputs

- current ACA distinction between verification and authorization;
- KES Mission Control critical path;
- EDS separation of evidence display from human decision;
- the proposed Authority Integrity Mechanics concepts developed by the Human Chief Architect.

## Required deliverables

1. Program charter.
2. Research and authority boundary.
3. Initial construct register.
4. Acceptance plan.
5. Mission Control update showing AIM-0000 as current authorized work and AIM-0001 as next eligible work.

## Constraints

- Research only; no operational scoring.
- No legal conclusions.
- No aggregate legitimacy or lawfulness percentage.
- Hard gates must remain distinct from descriptive profiles.
- Every construct must be labeled as candidate, validated, rejected, or retired.
- Every future measurement must preserve provenance, assumptions, units where applicable, and uncertainty.
- Verification may not be represented as authorization.

## Acceptance criteria

AIM-0000 is acceptable only if:

1. its mission and non-goals are explicit;
2. its authority boundary is visible in every primary document;
3. promotion into ACA requires a separate governed decision;
4. the anti-score and hard-gate rules are explicit;
5. the construct register distinguishes definitions from hypotheses;
6. AIM-0001 is blocked until this charter receives human acceptance;
7. no code claims to measure authority at this stage.

## Verification

Verification for AIM-0000 is documentary. Review must confirm that all required files exist, required boundary statements are present, and no prohibited operational claim is made.

## Completion condition

Human acceptance is recorded in the pull request. Merge makes the charter part of the repository; it does not validate any proposed mechanic.
