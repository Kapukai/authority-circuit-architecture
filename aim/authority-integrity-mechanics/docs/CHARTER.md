# AIM-0000 — Authority Integrity Mechanics Program Charter

## Status

Research candidate. Non-normative. Not authorized for operational scoring, legal conclusions, adverse action, publication as a standard, or autonomous decision-making.

## Mission

Discover whether exercises of authority exhibit measurable structural properties that can be observed, tested, challenged, and reproduced.

The program studies support relationships among:

- authority capacity;
- authority load;
- evidence capacity;
- liberty burden;
- reversibility;
- remedy capacity;
- residual uncertainty;
- authority safety margin.

These are research constructs, not established legal or scientific facts.

## Core research question

Can a proposed exercise of authority be structurally supported under the available evidence and the foreseeable burden, uncertainty, reversibility, and remedy conditions?

## Program objectives

1. Define observable primitives without pretending that interpretation is measurement.
2. Separate hard safety gates from descriptive profiles.
3. Preserve source provenance, uncertainty, and competing interpretations.
4. Produce falsifiable hypotheses and reproducible protocols.
5. Identify conditions under which a proposed metric becomes invalid or unsafe.
6. Prevent research outputs from being mistaken for authorization or legal judgment.
7. Establish a controlled path from research to possible future ACA standardization.

## Non-goals

AIM does not:

- decide legality, liability, guilt, entitlement, or jurisdiction;
- authorize action;
- rank human worth or credibility;
- infer intent from outcomes alone;
- collapse disputed facts into a single score;
- produce a percentage of lawfulness;
- allow favorable factors to average away a critical gate failure;
- replace domain experts, affected persons, adjudicators, or accountable human decision-makers.

## Research layers

### Layer 1 — Canonical Observation Record

A structured record of supplied observations, sources, provenance, timestamps, disputes, missing information, and transformations.

### Layer 2 — Independent Measurements

Separately computed candidate measurements with explicit assumptions and uncertainty. Measurements must not silently depend on one another.

### Layer 3 — Integrity Synthesis

A structured comparison of hard gates, profiles, contradictions, and uncertainty. Synthesis is explanatory, not authorizing.

### Layer 4 — Independent Verification

Reproduction by an implementation, analyst, or team that did not generate the original result.

### Layer 5 — Human Determination

An accountable human decides whether any action, further inquiry, pilot, publication, or standardization is authorized.

## Promotion gates

An AIM construct may be proposed for ACA consideration only after:

1. operational definition;
2. reference cases and counterexamples;
3. inter-rater or inter-implementation reproducibility;
4. sensitivity and uncertainty analysis;
5. adversarial attempts to break or manipulate it;
6. domain-expert review;
7. affected-rights and misuse review;
8. documented failure modes and rollback conditions;
9. explicit human acceptance of the evidence package.

Passing these gates does not itself create a standard. ACA standardization requires a separate work order and decision record.

## Governing invariant

Hard gates first. Profiles second. Critical failures cannot be averaged away.

## Authority boundary

Verification establishes that a declared method produced a declared result under stated conditions. It does not establish truth, legality, legitimacy, or permission to act.
