# AIM Initial Construct Register

All entries are candidate research constructs. Inclusion does not establish validity.

| Construct | Candidate meaning | Current state | Primary validation question |
|---|---|---|---|
| Authority Capacity | The supportable scope and intensity of action under stated authority, evidence, process, and safeguards. | CANDIDATE | Can independent reviewers identify the same support limits from the same record? |
| Authority Load | The scope, intensity, duration, reach, and irreversibility of a proposed exercise of authority. | CANDIDATE | Can load dimensions be observed without embedding a legal conclusion? |
| Unsupported Authority Load | The portion of a proposed load not supported under the declared capacity model. | CANDIDATE | Is the identified mismatch reproducible and sensitive to missing evidence? |
| Evidence Capacity | The degree to which supplied evidence can support specified factual propositions under explicit provenance and uncertainty conditions. | CANDIDATE | Can support be represented without converting disputed claims into facts? |
| Liberty Burden | The foreseeable restriction, deprivation, exposure, or constraint imposed on affected persons or rights. | CANDIDATE | Can burdens be represented across duration, severity, reach, and reversibility without ranking human worth? |
| Reversibility | The degree to which effects can be stopped, undone, corrected, or contained within a defined period and cost. | CANDIDATE | Are rollback conditions objectively observable? |
| Remedy Capacity | The practical ability of affected persons and responsible institutions to detect, challenge, correct, compensate, and prevent recurrence. | CANDIDATE | Can nominal remedy be distinguished from accessible and timely remedy? |
| Residual Uncertainty | Material uncertainty remaining after declared evidence collection and verification. | CANDIDATE | Can uncertainty sources and decision sensitivity be preserved rather than hidden? |
| Authority Safety Margin | The separation between declared support capacity and proposed load after uncertainty and safeguard assumptions. | HYPOTHESIS | Does any stable, non-misleading margin representation survive adversarial testing? |

## State vocabulary

- `CANDIDATE` — proposed for definition and testing.
- `HYPOTHESIS` — proposed relationship requiring empirical or formal evaluation.
- `VALIDATED` — passed the declared validation protocol within a specified scope.
- `REJECTED` — failed validation or was shown to be unsafe or incoherent.
- `RETIRED` — removed from active use because assumptions, scope, or evidence changed.

No construct may move to `VALIDATED` through author declaration alone.
