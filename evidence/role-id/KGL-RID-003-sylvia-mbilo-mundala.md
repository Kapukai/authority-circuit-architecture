## Supported use - Kapukai analysis

**Official authorship is not sufficient proof that a recorded event happened.** This case is relevant to the input-record stage of public decision systems and to verification methods that can challenge an official entry.

The next research question is whether, and how, a false entry affected a subsequent decision. The cited announcement does not trace a removal, placement, termination of parental rights, or other custody decision to these records. It also does not establish an institutional preference for an official's account over a family's account, a financial incentive causing the conduct, or a software defect.

The investigation and reported disposition belong in the accountability record. They do not establish whether affected records were corrected, downstream decisions reconsidered, or remedies provided to families. A criminal disposition and a remedy for an affected family are separate outcomes.
